2.1Liberty Mutual Insurance

Remote. May 2022 to present. Three titles in four and a half years.

2.1.1Principal Software Engineer

October 2025 to present

Paying claims with fewer people in the loop.

  1. Invoicing and payments. Co-lead a team of five engineers that launched straight-through invoicing for property claims. Vendor estimates are validated by the rules engine, invoiced and sent for payment with reduced manual review. On pace to process nearly $90M a year across 24,000 invoices, 45% of them eligible for auto-pay.
  2. Event-driven on Kafka. The service runs in the auto-scaling property platform. It consumes payment confirmations, downstream errors and coverage-status updates, and rechecks pending coverage after 72 hours.
  3. On-call and auth. On the rotation for all live services. Built the invoicing observability in Splunk and Datadog, and configured authentication and authorization for every service the team has set up: OAuth 2.0 across microservices, JWT where roles are needed.
  4. Claim linking. Technical lead for a human-in-the-loop workflow that links first- and third-party claims filed separately for the same incident. Reviewers confirm or reject candidate links from the data science team, and the results feed reporting.
  5. AI prototype. Built a RAG pipeline and a LangGraph multi-agent workflow for estimate review as an innovation effort.
  6. Mentoring. Mentor engineers in design and code reviews, and work with architects and business stakeholders on technical strategy and cost tradeoffs.

2.1.2Senior Software Engineer

July 2023 to October 2025

Turning fuzzy business policy into rules a machine can run.

  1. Rules engine. Designed a configurable engine that turns loosely defined policy into decision logic for adjuster skill routing. It has since scaled to three teams, 41 rule sets and 90 rules, at about 600,000 requests a year. In catastrophes such as hurricanes, floods and wildfires, its skill rules are reconfigured so adjusters are not overloaded.
  2. Security fix. Removed an expression-injection risk by replacing config-defined SpEL rules with annotated Java functions registered at startup.
  3. Vendor retirement. Led the retirement of a deeply embedded third-party scheduling platform across three engineering teams, finishing before the shutdown date with no downtime.

2.1.3Software Engineer

May 2022 to July 2023

Letting customers book their own inspections.

  1. Self-scheduling platform. Designed and led development of the core backend (Spring REST API, DynamoDB) and observability for a high-availability platform where customers scheduled property inspections, across three teams. Over about two years it replaced a manual workflow on hundreds of thousands of claims, saving an estimated $9M a year.
  2. Latency. Cut requests from 3 to 4 seconds down to about 100 ms by parallelizing downstream calls and deferring the Microsoft Graph calendar update until after the response.
  3. Cybersecurity Guardians. Selected for a cross-team group that finds security gaps and routes them to the teams building company-wide security foundations.
